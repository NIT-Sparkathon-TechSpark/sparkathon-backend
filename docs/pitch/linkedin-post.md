# LinkedIn post kit (Community Engagement, 10%)

Rules from the brief: the post must be **public**, include **our pitch**, **tag the judges, NiT and the sponsor accounts**, use **#NiTSparkathon**, and mention the **NiT Sparkathon** and **NIT-HH** pages. Only posts made **after 09:00 Friday** count. Counting stops at **14:00**; send a screenshot that shows the clock (by 14:05 at the latest) plus the link, no later than 16:00.

Scoring: the most-liked team gets 5, the least-liked gets 1. That's 10% of the final score, and it can decide a close finish.

## Plan
1. **09:00 sharp:** one team member publishes the post (the person with the largest network).
2. **09:00–09:15:** the other three like it, then reshare with a one-line personal comment. Each reshare reaches a new network.
3. **Ask people personally**: course mates, family, colleagues, alumni groups. Send the link directly with a two-line message (below). Direct messages convert far better than a feed post.
4. **Post the video version** if possible: a 30–45 s screen recording of the phone flow and `/live` filling up (from P3's backup video). Native video gets more reach than a link.
5. **13:55:** screenshot the post with the system clock visible. Send it with the link to the organisers.
